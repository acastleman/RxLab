    Lab label sheet: Width/Height are the peel-off label; Label left/top are measured from the sheet's left and top edges to the label's corner. To find them, print the alignment test on plain paper, lay it over a label sheet against a window, and read the label's edges off the rulers. Then print the test on a real label sheet: the dashed box should sit on the label's edges. If it is off, change Label left/top by the amount it missed. The auxiliary column (three 1.5 x 3/8 in labels above one 1.5 x 7/8 in label) is set the same way: Aux left/top is the top-left corner of the first small label, and Aux gap is the space between labels. Leave Sheet height at 11 unless the printer asks for the paper size.
    Printing uses the browser's print dialog. On a label printer, choose that printer, set margins to None and scale to 100 the first time. Use Scale % here to shrink or enlarge everything on the label if the printer trims an edge.
