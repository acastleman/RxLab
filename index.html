RxLab

Prescription entry and labeling for pharmacy skills and compounding labs, in one HTML file. Nothing to install.

Try the demo Source and set-up on GitHub

What it does

  • Students enter a prescription; picklists fill prescriber and product details, with look-alike entries to catch mismatches.
  • Sig codes are written out into directions that students read and edit before printing.
  • A live label check flags any of 14 required label elements that are missing.
  • Prints the label and auxiliary labels on your existing label stock, marked “For educational use only.”
  • Optional course database: enter on a laptop, print on a lab PC with a pickup code, and grade every save.

The demo

The demo runs standalone: sign in with any email, and everything you enter stays in your browser. It uses fictional prescribers and practice compounds.
